## Questions

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| 1 | List all films that have **at least two actors**. |
| 2 | List all directors who have directed **exactly two films**. |
| 3 | List all actors and **how many films** they appeared in, ordered by most appearances. |
| 4 | List all films ordered by **number of reviews**, from highest to lowest. |
| 5 | List all films ordered by **average rating**, from lowest to highest. |
| 6 | List films in which a person was **both an actor and the director**. |
| 7 | List all **female actors** ordered by date of birth. |
| 8 | Calculate the **age in years** of every director. |
| 9 | Find the **top 3 actors** who have worked with the highest number of different directors. |
| 10 | Find the **top 6 films** with the shortest title (ties broken by earliest release date). |
| 11 | Count the number of films per director released **between 1990 and 2010**. |
