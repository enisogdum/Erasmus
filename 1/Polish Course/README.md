# 🇵🇱 Polish Language and Culture 1

This repository contains my course materials, notes, and exercises from **Polish Language and Culture 1**, taken during my **Erasmus+ exchange at the West Pomeranian University of Technology (ZUT)** in Szczecin, Poland.

## 📚 Course Information

- **Course:** Polish Language and Culture 1
- **Course Code:** WM-SPNJO-P1
- **ECTS:** 4
- **Level:** A1
- **Language:** Polish
- **Duration:** 60 hours

## 🎯 Topics Covered

- Polish alphabet and pronunciation
- Greetings and introductions
- Numbers and basic personal information
- Food, drinks, colours, and everyday vocabulary
- People, nationalities, professions, and hobbies
- Family and relationships
- Interests, sports, and leisure
- Basic Polish grammar and sentence structures
- Polish culture, festivals, and traditions

## 📝 Assessment

- Practical classes
- Group work
- Listening comprehension
- Dialogues
- Text-based activities
- Continuous assessment
- Written exam
