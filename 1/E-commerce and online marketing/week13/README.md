# week13: Keyword Research & SEO Competitor Analysis

## Task Overview
Perform basic keyword research and competitor organic traffic analysis using the free tools provided by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) (no registration required).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **🌱 Formulate Keyword Seeds:**
    *   Write down **5 keyword seeds** (short phrases representing core search concepts) that describe your e-commerce business.
    *   Provide a short introductory paragraph reminding the reader about your webshop's niche and concept.

2.  **🔍 Target Market Selection:**
    *   Select your primary operating market/region (e.g., United States, Poland, United Kingdom) for your search queries.

3.  **📈 Keyword Generation:**
    *   Input your 5 keyword seeds into the [Ahrefs Keyword Generator](https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator).
    *   Analyze the output and take screenshots of the top search query results for each seed.
    *   Select **10 specific target keywords** (drawn from the generated results across all seeds).

4.  **💪 Keyword Difficulty Check:**
    *   Check each of the 10 selected keywords using the [Ahrefs Keyword Difficulty Checker](https://ahrefs.com/keyword-difficulty/).
    *   Take screenshots of the keyword difficulty index and estimated search volume for each keyword.

5.  **⚔️ Competitor Analysis:**
    *   Identify **3 active competitors** operating in the same industry/geographical market.
    *   Input their website domains into the [Ahrefs Traffic Checker](https://ahrefs.com/traffic-checker/) to estimate their monthly organic search traffic.
    *   Take a screenshot of the traffic checker results for each competitor.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Combine all elements (introductory webshop info, keyword seed screenshots, keyword difficulty screenshots, and competitor traffic screenshots) into a single PDF report.
*   Upload the report PDF to the assignment portal.
