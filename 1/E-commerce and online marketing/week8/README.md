# week8: Order Simulation & Management in WooCommerce

## Task Overview
Simulate client behavior by placing orders from different locations, and then practice order management workflows inside the WooCommerce administration dashboard.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Stage I: Order Placement Simulation
1.  **🛒 Place 5 Client Orders:**
    *   Place at least **5 separate orders**.
    *   Each order must contain at least **2 different items**.
    *   Distribute the orders among at least **3 different customer profiles/accounts**.
    *   Set the customers in different geographical locations (covering the different shipping zones configured in week6).
    *   *Tip:* Use the **Cash on Delivery (COD)** payment gateway to bypass real payment steps. Use [Temp-Mail](https://temp-mail.org) for faster customer registrations.
    *   *Screenshot:* Take a screenshot of the order confirmation/details page for each of the 5 orders showing the items ordered.

2.  **🔍 Order Verification:**
    *   Log in to the WordPress admin panel.
    *   Navigate to **WooCommerce > Orders** and verify that all 5 orders are listed and the details match what was ordered.
    *   *Screenshot:* Take a screenshot showing the list of all orders.
    *   *Screenshot:* Take a screenshot of the detailed item lists inside each individual order page.

---

### Stage II: Order Management
1.  **➕ Manually Create Orders:**
    *   Manually add **2 additional orders** from the WordPress admin panel.
    *   *Screenshot:* Take a screenshot of the WooCommerce orders dashboard showcasing the manually added orders.

2.  **⚙️ Edit & Process Orders:**
    *   Practice managing the orders by editing their details:
        *   **Status updates:** Transition order statuses (e.g., from *Processing* to *Completed*, *On-hold*, or *Cancelled*).
        *   **Detail adjustments:** Modify order details such as billing/shipping addresses, item quantities, or prices.
        *   **Order Notes:** Introduce custom order notes to document the modifications you performed.
    *   *Screenshot:* Take **Before & After** screenshots of the orders you modified to highlight the changes.

---
