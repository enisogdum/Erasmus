# week1: E-commerce Site Analysis

## Task Overview
Select your **3 favorite e-commerce sites** representing different categories:
1. **A Trading Entity** (e.g., retail, direct sales shop)
2. **A Service-Provider** (e.g., booking, SaaS, digital services)
3. **An Information/News Platform** (e.g., monetized news, content hub)

Perform a comprehensive analysis of each selected website.

---

## Analysis Requirements
For each of the three chosen sites, your analysis must cover the following aspects:

*   **📍 Location:** Country, region of origin/headquarters.
*   **📦 Offering:** What the company offers, including any limitation of items or focus on a specific industry.
*   **🌐 Operating Market:** The geographical/business scale on which the site operates (local, national, global).
*   **👥 Customers:** Target audience and focus on any particular demographic or customer group.
*   **💰 Source of Income:** All identifiable revenue models (direct sales, subscriptions, ads, commission, etc.).
*   **❤️ Rationale:** Why you personally like this particular website.
*   **📸 Visual Evidence:** **2-3 screenshots** showcasing key user interface elements or unique features.
