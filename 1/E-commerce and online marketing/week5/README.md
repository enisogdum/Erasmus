# week5: WooCommerce Catalog Configuration

## Task Overview
Configure the basic inventory structure of your local WooCommerce store by defining product taxonomies (categories, tags) and adding products. 

*Tip: You can reuse the product descriptions, assets, and catalog information gathered during the Shopify exercise in week3.*

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **📦 Identify Products (Select 10 items):**
    *   Select **10 products** that represent your brand and target market.
    *   Ensure a diverse range of products across different categories to make the inventory robust.

2.  **🗂️ Create Categories:**
    *   Define clear product categories (e.g., *Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Fashion*) to organize your items effectively.
    *   Categories should represent the main segments of your store's catalog.

3.  **🏷️ Define Tags:**
    *   Create tags for more specific filtering and product categorization within your main categories (e.g., *eco-friendly, best seller, new arrival, limited edition*).

4.  **⚙️ Set Up Taxonomies & Products:**
    *   Refer to the [WooCommerce Product Taxonomies Documentation](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-product-taxonomies/) to set up your categories and tags.
    *   For product creation and inventory options, review the [WooCommerce Managing Products Documentation](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-products/).

5.  **📸 Documentation:**
    *   Capture clear screenshots of each category, tag, and product setup inside the WooCommerce admin panel.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Compile all your administrative screenshots into a single PDF document.
*   Upload the PDF report to the assignment portal.
