# week10: Email Newsletter Campaign Design

## Task Overview
Design and create a marketing newsletter for your webshop announcing a promotional event or store update to drive engagement and encourage customer retention.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **💡 Select a Topic:**
    *   Choose one of the following engaging topics:
        *   **Webshop grand opening:** Announce launch, opening offers, and shop hours.
        *   **New product line:** Highlight key products, features, benefits, and launch date.
        *   **Seasonal promotion:** Limited-time holiday or seasonal sale.
        *   **Back-in-stock / restock event:** Highlight popular returning items.
        *   **Exclusive members’ event:** Loyalty perks, early access, or coupon codes.
        *   **Collaboration / limited drop:** Designer collaboration or capsule collection.
        *   **How-to / gift guide:** Curated picks for a specific audience.

2.  **🎨 Choose a Design Tool:**
    *   Create your newsletter using one of the following formats:
        *   *Web-based design tool:* Canva.com or Figma.com (Highly Recommended)
        *   *Graphics editor:* Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, GIMP, etc.
        *   *Text document:* Microsoft Word, LibreOffice Writer, etc.

3.  **📐 Required Newsletter Elements:**
    Your newsletter design must include all of the following elements:
    *   **Headline:** 6–10 words.
    *   **Subheadline:** 8–15 words.
    *   **Hero Image:** A high-quality visual representation of your theme.
    *   **Intro:** 1–3 introductory sentences.
    *   **Key Details:** Clear information regarding *what*, *when* (dates/times), *who* benefits, *where* to shop, and *how* to participate.
    *   **Featured Products:** 3–6 featured products/offers, each accompanied by a short blurb + price or discount.
    *   **CTA (Call-To-Action):** A distinctive button or text action (e.g., *Shop the Sale Now*).
    *   **Social Proof:** 1–2 customer testimonials or rating metrics.
    *   **Footer:** Essential info including your webshop URL, contact email/phone, social media handles, and an unsubscribe option.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Export your finished newsletter design as a **PDF** or **PNG** file.
*   Upload the exported file to the assignment portal.
