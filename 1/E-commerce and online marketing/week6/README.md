# week6: Shipping Configuration & Cost Structure

## Task Overview
Define a shipping and delivery cost structure for your WooCommerce webshop. You will research courier services, set up five shipping zones, configure flat-rate delivery costs, and document the configuration.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **🚚 Identify Courier Services:**
    *   Choose parcel/courier services (e.g., DHL, DPD, FedEx, local postal services) available in the primary operating country of your webshop.
    *   Research their pricing models, range of services, and estimated delivery times.
    *   *Report Content:* Document which couriers you analyzed, providing reasons why you selected or rejected them.

2.  **🗺️ Identify Shipping Zones:**
    *   Define at least **five distinct shipping zones/countries** where your webshop will deliver products.

3.  **📊 Create a Cost Configuration Table:**
    *   Create a summary table containing:
        *   Shipping zone/country
        *   Courier service selected
        *   Shipping cost (Flat fee)
        *   Estimated delivery times

4.  **⚙️ Configure WooCommerce Shipping:**
    *   Navigate to your local WooCommerce settings and configure shipping zones.
    *   Add your five defined shipping zones and assign a flat-rate shipping fee to each zone according to your cost table.
    *   Refer to the [WooCommerce Shipping Zones Setup Guide](https://woocommerce.com/document/setting-up-shipping-zones/) for detailed steps.

5.  **📸 Documentation:**
    *   Take screenshots of your completed WooCommerce shipping zones and flat-rate cost configurations.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Create a report PDF containing your courier analysis, cost table, and administrative configuration screenshots.
*   Upload the report PDF to this assignment.

---

## Notes & Advanced Context
*   For simplicity, this task uses a **fixed/flat fee** for shipping that does not take into account internal packaging, labor, or item dimensions/weight/quantity.
*   *Real-World Tip:* If you ever need weight-based or dynamic shipping costs in a production WooCommerce store, you will need to utilize additional plugins like [WooCommerce Weight Based Shipping](https://wordpress.org/plugins/weight-based-shipping-for-woocommerce/).
