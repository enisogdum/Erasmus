# week7: Tax Configuration (VAT) in WooCommerce

## Task Overview
Configure standard sales tax (VAT) for your WooCommerce store across multiple geographical locations. You will research VAT rates, implement tax rules in WooCommerce, adjust pricing parameters if needed, and document the configuration.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **📊 Identify VAT Rates (At least 5 locations):**
    *   Select at least **5 countries** (for simplicity, EU member states work best) and identify their current standard VAT rates.
    *   Refer to the [Official EU VAT Rules & Rates Guide](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/taxation/vat/vat-rules-rates/index_en.htm) under the "VAT rates applied in EU member countries" section.

2.  **⚙️ Configure WooCommerce Tax Settings:**
    *   Enable tax calculations inside WooCommerce general settings.
    *   Add your researched tax configurations for the 5 selected countries.
    *   Adjust store prices accordingly (decide whether to enter prices inclusive or exclusive of tax).
    *   Refer to the [WooCommerce Set Up Taxes Manual](https://woocommerce.com/document/setting-up-taxes-in-woocommerce/) and the [WooCommerce General Tax Documentation](https://woocommerce.com/documentation/woocommerce/taxes/) for guide details.

3.  **📸 Verify Configuration:**
    *   Take screenshots of the price breakdown on the product page or checkout flow for **5 products**, showing how the different VAT rates are calculated and displayed based on the buyer's location.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Compile the images into a PDF report.
*   Submit the completed PDF.

---

## Additional Information
*   **EU VAT eCommerce Changes:** Read more about [2021 EU VAT eCommerce changes](https://woocommerce.com/document/eu-vat-july-2021-changes-ecommerce-and-woocommerce/).
*   **Automated Taxes:** For production stores, merchants often use services like [WooCommerce Tax](https://woocommerce.com/products/tax/) to dynamically automate global tax calculations.
