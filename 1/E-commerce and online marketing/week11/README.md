# week11: Social Media Strategy & Channel Analysis

## Task Overview
Assess how different companies utilize diverse social media channels to engage with various audience segments. This analysis helps in understanding modern digital marketing strategies and cross-platform communication patterns.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **🏢 Select 3 Companies:**
    *   Choose **3 companies** belonging to the same industry.
    *   Avoid choosing highly specialized companies that focus on a very narrow customer group.
    *   The selected companies should have active presences across multiple social media platforms.

2.  **🔍 Identify Official Profiles:**
    *   Find, verify the authenticity of, and list the official accounts of each company on major platforms:
        *   Facebook
        *   Twitter (X)
        *   Instagram
        *   LinkedIn
        *   YouTube
        *   TikTok
        *   *Any other industry-relevant platforms.*

3.  **📊 Analyze Content Across Channels:**
    For each platform used by the companies, analyze the following aspects:
    *   **Type of Content:** What is the primary focus of the posts (e.g., promotional, educational, user-generated, entertainment)? What media formats are used (images, videos, text, infographics)?
    *   **Corporate Message & Tone:** What is the brand voice/tone (formal, casual, humorous, professional)? How do they tailor messages to different audiences (e.g., consumers vs. job-seekers/investors)?
    *   **Engagement Metrics:** Observe user engagement indicators (likes, shares, comments, retweets, views) to gauge audience response.

4.  **📸 Screenshots & Descriptions:**
    *   Capture screenshots of each company's profile homepages and example posts that represent their strategy.
    *   Write a descriptive summary for each channel explaining the effectiveness of their strategy for that platform's demographic (e.g., TikTok for Gen Z vs. LinkedIn for professionals).

5.  **📝 Synthesis & Comparison:**
    *   Summarize your overall findings.
    *   Identify similarities, differences, and unique strengths/weaknesses in how the three companies manage their digital presence.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Export your complete analysis report, including screenshots and comparisons, as a PDF.
*   Submit the PDF report to the assignment portal.
