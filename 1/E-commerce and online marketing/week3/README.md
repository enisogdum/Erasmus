# week3: Product Definition on Shopify

## Task Overview
Add products to your Shopify store, ensuring they are rich in details and aligned with your webshop's theme.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **🔑 Account Setup:**
    *   Due to Shopify's limited trial periods, you will need to open a new test account.
    *   Use [Temp-Mail](https://temp-mail.org/) to quickly register a new temporary email address if needed.

2.  **📦 Define Products:**
    *   Add at least **10 products** to your Shopify store.
    *   Provide as much detail as possible for each product (e.g., titles, rich descriptions, pricing, sizes, inventory levels, etc.).
    *   *Suggestion:* Locate real-world products online and fetch high-quality media (images, videos) provided by their manufacturers.
    *   Ensure the product range remains coherent with your store's concept/niche chosen in week2.

3.  **📸 Documentation:**
    *   Take a screenshot of each of the 10 products as they appear in your Shopify webshop.
    *   Combine all 10 screenshots into a single PDF document.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Create a single PDF document (e.g., `ec-week3.pdf`) containing the 10 product screenshots.
*   Upload the PDF report to this assignment.
*   ⚠️ *Reminder: Stay mindful of Shopify's short trial duration.*
