# week2: Shopify Webshop Setup

## Task Overview
Start an experience with a hosted web shop using **Shopify**, one of the leading e-commerce platforms.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1.  **💡 Concept Development:**
    *   Think about your webshop idea: select a business branch/niche, range of products to be sold, and your target audience.
    *   Provide a short (1-2 paragraph) explanation of this concept in your report.

2.  **🛒 Shopify Shop Creation:**
    *   Create a test Shopify store.
    *   Define all necessary shop information (title, location, contact details, etc.).
    *   *Tip:* You can use a temporary email service like [Temp-Mail](https://temp-mail.org) for registration.

3.  **⚙️ Admin Panel Exploration:**
    *   Experiment with the Shopify administration tool to understand how it functions. Refer to the official [Shopify Admin Overview Guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/shopify-admin-overview) for help.

4.  **🎨 Theme Selection & Customization:**
    *   Test at least **5 different themes** for your store.
    *   Take a screenshot of your store configured with each theme.
    *   Select one final theme.
    *   Provide an explanation for each of the 5 tested themes, justifying why they were rejected or why the final one was selected.

5.  **🌐 Publish & Share:**
    *   Publish your webshop so that it is accessible to external users who are not logged in.
    *   Provide the public URL to access your shop.

---

## Deliverables & Submission
*   Upload your final report containing the store concept, theme analysis (with screenshots), and store URL to the assignment portal.
